Play Weatherbound ↗

Project Overview

As a wizard, you can use your environmental spells to defeat enemies and collect the 3 keys in order to fight and defeat the evil wizard!

My Contributions

I was tasked with giving each of the enemies their own unique programming. Goblins run slowly towards the player and do heavy damage, skeletons run quickly towards the player and do light damage, and goblin archers stay at a distance and shoot arrows at the player, dealing moderate damage. These enemies are prefabs that can be duplicated and placed throughout each of the levels in this game.
