Poorly Maintained Road Simulator

Unity • C#

See how long you can drive on a poorly maintained road!

Currently no game build as of now, will update later.

Project Overview

In this game, you take a drive down a very poorly maintained road! Your goal is to survive as long as possible as your car progressively gets faster! Avoid obstacles across 4 lanes and see how long you last!

My Contributions

I was responsible for programming the endless road tile spawner. This system randomly selects 1 of 20 made road prefabs from a configurable array and continuously generates new segments ahead of the player while removing segments behind the player. The player does not move at all, the road tiles below move, and the player can switch between the 4 lanes from left to right. The tiles are aligned using prefab exit points, configurable spawn and despawn distances, and a separate speed controller that supports a delayed start and gradual acceleration. This array system can support interchangable road prefabs that feature individual lane terrain textures, environmental scenery, and obstacle placements. This allows for additional content to be integrated without modifying the core spawning logic.
